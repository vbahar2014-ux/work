⚡ Pattern of the Day
The offense is converging on the boxes that guard and manage the estate — and this week it got a public toolkit for one of them.

Issue #67, covering the ~24 hours since Issue #66. The exploitation wave did not slow — it broadened onto the network-appliance tier. CISA opened a second same-week KEV batch with a federal deadline of today, 12 September, adding an exploited Cisco Secure FMC auth-bypass (CVE-2026-20079, CVSS 10.0 → root) and an exploited Fortinet FortiOS flaw (CVE-2025-25249) now delivering a purpose-built RAT ("PivotC2") to FortiGate devices — alongside the Citrix NetScaler CVE-2026-19490 already in that batch. Three of the four are the network's own control surfaces: firewall management plane, firewall data plane, and remote-access ADC. Compromise one and the attacker sits above the controls.

The NetScaler case shows the tempo: disclosed in August, exploited in early September, and publicly weaponized with a drop-in detection tool by 10 September (Bishop Fox) — the disclosure-to-tooling interval now measured in days. Against that, AG's own patch reflex is partly jammed: the September Windows cumulative still has no fix for the Remote Desktop Services regression (no KIR as of 10 Sept), even as it carries two exploited privilege-escalation zero-days. The EU CRA's 24-hour reporting duty, meanwhile, is now simply in force.

For Group Close the 12 September KEV appliance batch today — Cisco FMC, FortiOS/FortiGate and NetScaler on fixed builds — and, because all three are exploited/weaponized, hunt rather than assume patched (FMC web shells; PivotC2 on FortiGate; NetScaler SSRF/NITRO/cron abuse, using the now-public one-request patch-state check). Hold the Windows RDS staged-deploy posture until Microsoft ships a KIR — the two exploited EoP zero-days still carry a 22 Sept deadline. Keep the management-plane backlog closed under assume-compromise (Chrome KEV 18 Sept, ~6 days). And hold the Dreamforce (15–17 Sept, ~3 days), memory-supercycle and EU AI Act 2 Dec marking (~80 days) watchpoints.
