⚡ Pattern of the Day
Agent governance breaks out of the hyperscaler control planes — into a multi-vendor layer riding open standards.

Yesterday the platforms (Microsoft Agent 365, ServiceNow, Okta) had turned governance into their product. Today the layer decouples: independent security vendors — Reco (12 Jun), Linx, Zenity, Grip — now ship agent governance built directly on the model provider, anchored on Anthropic's Compliance API, and Cloudflare ships agent identity at the network edge on the W3C Web Bot Auth standard. Microsoft's Work IQ context plane goes GA in three days, metered by Copilot Credits.

So the single decision Group faced yesterday — which control plane — splits into three coupled choices: context plane + governance vendor + identity standard. The upside: emerging open standards (Web Bot Auth, A2A, MCP) finally offer a way to make them without surrendering to one vendor's gravity — even as the EU clock ticks to ~50 days.

For Group Pick the agent-identity + governance standard deliberately, biased toward open standards (Web Bot Auth / A2A / MCP) over any single control plane — and keep the August GPAI + Article 50 readiness fully funded against a date that refuses to slip.
