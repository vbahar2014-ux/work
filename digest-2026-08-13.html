⚡ Pattern of the Day
The agent stack just acquired its three missing pieces — identity, a settlement rail, and a power mandate.

Since 2 August the EU AI Act has crossed from deadline to live enforcement: ~11 days in, the AI Office is actively supervising GPAI (fines up to €15M / 3% turnover) and Article 50 is in force, with high-risk Annex III duties deferred to 2 Dec 2027 under Reg. (EU) 2026/1744. In the same window the governance and commerce layers hardened into product: Cequence adds Agent Personas binding an agent's job description to its model, tools and guardrails — sharpening AG's buy-vs-build control-plane call.

Cloudflare gives agents Wallets + cloudflare.pay so machine-majority traffic can transact under delegated, capped spend; Adtran ships an operator-owned agent fabric on MCP; and Google's 12 Aug Gemini Automation re-bases the interaction layer on agent-driven navigation. Meanwhile the physical constraint became policy: FERC's 60-day mandate lands mid-August (data centers pay full interconnection) even as off-grid deals (Energy Vault, 1.25 GW) route around the queue. Control, settlement and power are each now productised or mandated.

For Group Two moves this cycle: (1) lock the agent control-plane + identity + persona/guardrail standard against the now-shipping Cequence / Cloudflare / Google options — and decide a posture on agent-initiated spend — before the next production rollout; and (2) operate GPAI + Article 50 in production under active AI-Office supervision, confirm the Annex III rephasing to 2 Dec 2027, and screen every system against the Article 5 prohibitions.
