⚡ Pattern of the Day
The deadline arrived — and the market shipped the control plane to meet it.

On 2 August the core EU AI Act obligations went live; five days on, GPAI enforcement and Article 50 transparency are in force, with high-risk Annex III duties still deferred to 2 Dec 2027 under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. In the same window the governance layer moved from principle to product: Cequence claims the first end-to-end agentic zero-trust ring across MCP, API and LLM — turning AG's open agent-control-plane buy-vs-build pilot into a concrete decision.

Cloudflare reports agent traffic +1,700% YoY with machines now over half its network, and shifts to a pay-per-use content economy (with a 15 September default block on mixed-use crawlers); the physical layer set a near-record — $22.3B of data-center construction starts in June, YTD spend already past all of 2025 — even as power constraints force some builds to be replanned and novel forms (Samsung's floating DCs) emerge. Gartner now names agentic NetOps a market of its own. Control, power and portability each just acquired a concrete product or price.

For Group Two moves this cycle: (1) decide the agent control-plane + identity standard against the now-shipping Cequence / Cloudflare options — don't let it be chosen by default before the next production rollout; and (2) confirm the Annex III rephasing to 2 Dec 2027 while keeping GPAI and Article 50 obligations operating in production and screening every system against the new Article 5 prohibitions.
