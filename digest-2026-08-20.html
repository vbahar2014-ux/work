⚡ Pattern of the Day
The soft constraints harden — power scarcity climbs from the statehouse to the grid operator, agent risk from the security team to the boardroom.

Two threads AG has tracked as emerging pressures both graduated into structural rules this cycle. On power, yesterday's story was two states pausing hyperscale approvals; today PJM — the largest US grid operator (13 states, ~67M people) — filed two plans with FERC (13 Aug) that would let big new loads energise ahead of capacity but hand states the authority to cut data centers off during grid stress unless they bring their own generation, backstopped by ~$20B in procured capacity, with a 12 Oct effective date. In parallel the PUCT's 20 Aug open meeting takes up ERCOT's good-cause exception after Batch Zero classifications were suspended — the decision point AG flagged yesterday, now live.

On agents, the SAP LeanIX Agentic AI Survey reframed sprawl as a board-level governance failure mode: adoption high, oversight thin, shadow agents outrunning controls, and the window to install governance before a serious incident closing — escalating yesterday's "the inventory gate is buyable" (Cequence) into "the board should be asking for it." Around these, portability moved down a layer — from the network (Tuesday's Apache-2.0 multicloud Interconnect) to the data tier, as Google Cloud DMS added Gemini-powered conversion of legacy database code into open PostgreSQL/AlloyDB. The sector narrative caught up: ~57% of organisations now run agents in production and Gartner projects >75% of EMEA enterprises sovereign by 2030. Regulation stayed the through-line — the EU AI Act is ~18 days into enforcement, with Article 50 emerging as the forcing function to audit every agent-generated output.

For Group Three moves: (1) extend US-capacity diligence to a curtailment-risk test — does the site have contracted/on-site power, and which state can order it cut? — treating PJM's 12 Oct effective date as the point this becomes a commercial term; (2) stand up AG's agent + MCP inventory and least-privilege baseline now, as a board-reportable control; and (3) operationalise Article 50 — mark every place AG's agents generate customer-facing output — while confirming which GPAI suppliers are Code-of-Practice signatories.
