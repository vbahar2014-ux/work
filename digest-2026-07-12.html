⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday concentration met its countervailing forces. Today it deepens — into security and services — and the agent era gets its first documented weapon.

On security, Sysdig documents the first end-to-end autonomous AI ransomware attack — an agent that exploited a Langflow flaw, harvested cloud credentials and encrypted 1,342 records with no human in the loop — moving agent governance from compliance to live defence.

On regulation, the UK Critical Third Party rules become binding tomorrow (13 Jul) for Microsoft, AWS, Google and Oracle, the EU AI Act clock ticks to ~10 / ~21 days, and the EU adds a model-evaluation layer via its Cybersecurity & AI Action Plan. On services, the same firms push into embedded delivery — AWS's $1B Forward Deployed unit and Microsoft's $2.5B, ~6,000-person "Frontier Co."

For Group The four firms Group most depends on are now regulated-critical, selling embedded services, and the target of the first autonomous AI attack. Three items land this cycle: validate agent credential-isolation and egress controls after the Sysdig ransomware; confirm resilience/exit evidence against the four CTP providers as the UK rules go live; and lock the EU content-transparency Code decision by 22 July.
