⚡ Pattern of the Day
The clock reached zero. As of today the EU AI Act can compel, evaluate and fine — the regulator moved from persuasion to compulsion.

The enforcement era begins: as of this morning the Commission's AI Office holds live powers over GPAI providers — documentation requests, direct model evaluations, corrective / withdrawal measures and fines up to €15M / 3% turnover (broader breaches €35M / 7%), and Article 50 chatbot / deepfake disclosure is in force. Enforcement begins into a market where ~78% of organisations had not taken meaningful compliance steps as of April.

The build-out closed its full cycle the same week: with Alphabet's Q2 now beside Amazon's and Microsoft's, all three US hyperscalers raised 2026 capex in one window and Google Cloud grew 82% to $24.8B on a $514B backlog. The counterweight hardened into a number: only ~5 GW of the ~16 GW of 2026-planned data-centre capacity is under construction, with interconnection queues reaching seven years.

For Group One action this cycle: confirm which of Group's foundation-model providers appear on the published signatory list (a proxy for Article 50 presumption-of-conformity), verify chatbot / deepfake disclosure is live in production today, and use the four-month marking grace (to 2 Dec 2026) to finish the AI-generated-content inventory to a multi-jurisdiction, agent-aware standard. Readiness is now measured against a live regulator, not a deadline. On the money: pursue committed-use / reserved-capacity terms against a still-climbing supplier spend curve, and build delivery-slip contingency into capacity planning.
