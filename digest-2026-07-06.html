⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday the constraint climbed to reliability and margin. Today the response layer forms on each — the grid-risk mechanism is documented, and compute cost shows up in the models AG actually picks.

On the physical layer, NERC's picture hardens from "alert issued" to documented mechanism: the ~1,800 MW February shed is now traced to data-centre UPS systems tripping offline during a normally cleared fault, and 2025's weighted equivalent forced-outage rate climbed to 9.2% (above the 7–8% band). The Aug 3 response deadline is now ~28 days — and hyperscaler "bring-your-own-power" deals (20-year Chevron–Microsoft, Microsoft's Wisconsin build) show self-generation moving from signal to contract.

On the economics layer, the pressure that showed up yesterday as vendor margin-capture now shows up in usage: low-cost non-US models have taken ~45% of a major routing layer's traffic in a year, and Gemini 3.5 Pro is flagged for burning more tokens than expected in enterprise preview. The agentic-governance plumbing, meanwhile, goes live on the API/edge plane today (Apigee Agent Management + MCP at API Horizon Munich).

For Group Firm-power siting must keep large-load ride-through diligence as a named acceptance criterion as the mechanism hardens; model-portability now has to cover non-US providers explicitly (compliance/security priced in) and require per-task token-cost benchmarking. Two items land this cycle: keep grid-stability diligence in capacity-siting reviews, and lock the EU AI Act content-transparency Code decision by 22 July.
