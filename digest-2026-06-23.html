⚡ Pattern of the Day
Today is the identity turn. Agent governance climbs from vendor product into state infrastructure — and sovereignty sinks from EU draft text into the substrate.

A week ago the agent stack started selling governance. Today Estonia became the first state to propose national ID codes for AI agents — unique, persistent, auditable, scoped — putting agent identity on the same registry as citizens and companies. The per-agent-identity baseline vendors converged on is now being drafted into law.

In parallel sovereignty stopped being only a Commission proposal: Huawei Cloud shipped it into the substrate (HYOK hardware encryption, data capsules, isolation as an "Agentic Infra" data-security zone), and Gartner put a 15× curve behind the pull — sub-5% to 75%+ of EMEA enterprises geopatriating by 2030. Power stayed the binding constraint, escalating from a watchdog warning into a federal bill — all against an AI Act clock at ~40 days.

For Group The most consequential near-term call is to design Group's agent-identity scheme now to be registry-ready against emerging sovereign agent-ID schemes (Estonia first, others likely to follow) — so Group's agents are registry-ready rather than retrofitted, and the August GPAI + Article 50 obligations land on existing controls.
