⚡ Pattern of the Day
The deadline the digest has counted down for weeks arrives today — and the compute story's binding constraint moves from money to the grid.

The EU AI Act clock reaches zero on the Code and enters its final week on enforcement: the content-transparency Code initial-signatory window closes at 18:00 CEST today (27 Jul); GPAI enforcement (fines up to €15M / 3% turnover), Article 50 marking and Member-State AI sandboxes all go live 2 August — now ~6 days out. That is the single item that crosses from awareness into decision this cycle.

Underneath it, the build-out's binding constraint shifts from capital to power: BloombergNEF nearly doubled its US data-centre power forecast to 194 GW by 2035 (~20% of US electricity; a 19 GW shortfall even at record build rates), and Oracle is in court over a Wisconsin collateral rule that could cost it ~$7B. Meanwhile the supply side reshapes — Meta enters as a fourth hyperscaler (Meta Compute, undercutting 20–30%) and Anthropic locks in AMD (up to 2 GW MI450, up to $5B) plus a ~$1.11T valuation ahead of an October IPO.

For Group One action this cycle: close the sign / don't-sign decision on the EU content-transparency Code before 18:00 CEST today, confirm whether Group's foundation-model providers sit on the GPAI Code signatory list, and finish the AI-generated-content inventory ahead of 2 August. The BloombergNEF forecast and the Oracle–Wisconsin fight sharpen the case for European/Gulf-primary siting of residency-sensitive workloads; the Meta Compute entrant adds burst/secondary optionality without displacing that thesis.
