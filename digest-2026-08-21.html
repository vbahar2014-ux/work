⚡ Pattern of the Day
The hardened constraints enter their machinery — the power rule opens its comment window, the Texas audit starts, and the agent controls become shipping product.

Yesterday's cycle was about two structural rules appearing: PJM's FERC filing and the SAP LeanIX survey reframing agent sprawl as a board-level failure mode. Today those threads begin operating. On power, FERC has docketed PJM's two plans and set a 3 September comment/intervention deadline ahead of the requested 12 Oct effective date, with PJM quantifying the gap it is closing — ~70 GW of new large-load demand by 2038 against ~15 GW of retirements since 2022. In Texas the PUCT's 20 Aug meeting has been held and ERCOT is launching its verification/audit "shortly after," splitting into a Batch Zero eligibility audit and a Community Impact audit (≥25 MW).

On agents, the board-level warning met the shipping control plane — Microsoft treats every agent as a governed identity (Agent 365 across Entra/Purview/Defender) and adds an Agentic Security Specialization for permission sprawl, while Identiverse practitioners call unmanaged sprawl a present reality. The network edge productised the same idea: Cloudflare's Agents Week shipped agent wallets, an Identity-Aware AI Gateway and MCP-traffic detection. Regulation sharpened — law-firm guidance crystallised Article 50's two-tier clock: live now for new systems, with a 2 December 2026 marking deadline for generative systems already on the market — as Gartner's 2026 top-10 gave the pattern its labels (geopatriation, digital provenance).

For Group Three moves: (1) treat the 3 Sept–12 Oct PJM window as the point curtailment becomes a commercial term — require self-supply/curtailment clauses in PJM-footprint commitments; (2) pick the agent-identity plane deliberately — standardise on agents-as-governed-identity with least privilege, pilot discovery (Agent 365, Cloudflare Identity-Aware Gateway) against existing logs, make the agent + MCP inventory board-reportable; and (3) tier the Article 50 rollout to its two dates — mark new agent output now, schedule the pre-existing generative estate against 2 Dec 2026.
