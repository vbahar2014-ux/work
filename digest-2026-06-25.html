⚡ Pattern of the Day
Today is the power-ceiling turn. The binding constraint surfaces on the physical floor — and it is the grid operator, not the regulator, who names it.

A day after FERC ordered a 90-day fast-track for data-centre grid access, PJM — the largest US grid, serving ~67M people across 13 states — formally warned it can't keep up, adding a new "capacity advisory" and projecting a ~6 GW reliability shortfall by 2027. Acceleration of demand has met a hard supply wall: power, not silicon or governance tooling, is becoming the dominant ceiling on the AI build-out.

The capital response is already visible and sovereignty-shaped — SoftBank's €75B / 5 GW across France, gigawatt campuses in Texas where power is available — while Huawei's INSPIRE shipped a sovereign agentic substrate as product and Gartner crystallised the demand side into a named behaviour: geopatriation ($80B sovereign IaaS, 75% of EMEA enterprises by 2030). AI Act clock at ~38 days.

For Group The most consequential near-term call is to build a grid-reliability / power-availability sensitivity into US-sited capacity planning and contracts — PJM's advisory makes curtailment and rate risk concrete — while keeping the full 2-August GPAI + Article 50 readiness programme funded.
