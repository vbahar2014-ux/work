⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday's abstractions became products and dates. Today the story is concentration meeting its countervailing forces — regulators name the hyperscalers critical while those same firms ship anti-lock-in tooling.

On regulation, a second jurisdiction sets a hard cloud deadline — the UK Treasury designates Microsoft, AWS, Google and Oracle as Critical Third Parties, with a 13 July (~2 days) cut-off to prove financial-sector resilience — while the EU AI Act clock ticks to ~11 days (content-transparency Code) and ~22 days (GPAI enforcement).

On the frontier, the Western labs answer the offshore price floor on efficiency — OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol tops the Coding Agent Index at 80 and runs 54% more token-efficient. And portability arrives as product: Google Cloud Next's Iceberg-based Cross-Cloud Lakehouse and AWS + Google's multicloud link-up let teams query data in place — even as 2026 DC capex nears $750B.

For Group The four firms Group most depends on are being pulled two ways — named critical by regulators, yet selling the tools to reduce lock-in. Two items land this cycle: treat hyperscaler concentration as a named, testable resilience risk (map critical services against the four CTP providers), and lock the EU content-transparency Code decision by 22 July — with Iceberg-standardised portability and delivered-cost-per-task benchmarking reaffirmed.
