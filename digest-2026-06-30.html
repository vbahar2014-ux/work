⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday everything got a number. Today the number moved up a level — from the AI budget to the sovereignty budget.

Gartner elevated geopatriation to a top-2026 strategic trend and put figures behind it: ~75% of European & Middle-Eastern enterprises forecast to repatriate workloads to sovereign or regional clouds by 2030 (from <5% in 2025), sovereign-cloud spend rising ~35.6% to ~$80B in 2026, and 61% of W-European/APAC CIOs now ranking geopolitical risk above cost — with Airbus tendering €50M+ for a sovereign EU cloud and ~51% of enterprises already hybrid-sovereign.

The agent thread hardened in parallel — not "you need governance" but "you can't switch it off." A Cloud Security Alliance / Token Security study finds 60% of enterprises cannot terminate a misbehaving agent and 63% cannot enforce purpose limitations, even as ~72% run agents in production — and OpenAI's GPT-5.4 just raised the native-computer-use autonomy ceiling. EU AI Act: Transparency Code published 10 Jun, signatory cut-off ~22 days, GPAI live ~33 days.

For Group Sovereign-first is now the mainstream default, not a contrarian hedge — and the mandate is explicitly sovereign AI. Lock the EU-resident landing-zone + data-residency standard this quarter, and make a runtime kill-switch + purpose-limitation baseline a non-negotiable gate before the next production agent rollout.
