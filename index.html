⚡ Pattern of the Day
The blast radius moves down the stack — from last week's identity plane to the virtualization and network edge the whole estate runs on.

Last week's sharpest threats hit identity (Entra's CVSS 10.0 RCE, the FortiGate/Claude Code intrusion). This week the same industrialised N-day playbook migrates one layer down. A single suspected China-nexus actor took a VMware vCenter path-traversal RCE (CVE-2026-59310, CVSS 9.8) that Broadcom disclosed 29 July, had live compromises by 3 August, and turned into root access, cron + Reverse SSH backdoors, directory-credential theft and Babuk-derived ransomware on ESXi across 47 countries (US and France among the most-targeted). In parallel, Windows IKE (CVE-2026-33824) — a CVSS 9.8 unauthenticated RCE reachable via crafted UDP to ports 500/4500 — is under exploitation by a Chinese-speaking actor running a partly AI-assisted (DeepSeek) campaign. Both, plus SharePoint and macOS flaws, were added to CISA KEV on 18 Aug with a federal patch deadline of 21 Aug that has now passed — "patch, then assume compromise."

The counter-current is constructive: the agent-standards layer consolidated — A2A formally joined the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation on 20 Aug, alongside MCP, 250+ members — confirming the three-part interoperability base AG's agent-procurement policy assumes. Behind both, the clocks tick: PJM's comment window closes in ~9 days (3 September), the EU AI Act marking clock is ~99 days out, and NVIDIA reports Q2 FY27 after the close tomorrow (26 Aug) — the near-term tell on whether the data-center capex cycle is still accelerating.

For Group Four moves: (1) patch and threat-hunt every internet-reachable vCenter and Windows IKE/IPsec endpoint now, treat anything exposed since ~3 Aug as potentially compromised, and confirm ESXi/backup isolation; (2) verify the wider KEV cluster (SharePoint, macOS) and close the FortiGate/Entra carryovers from Issue #51; (3) hold the two live deadlines — PJM self-supply/curtailment clauses before 3 Sept and the EU AI Act marking schedule to 2 Dec 2026; and (4) require A2A + MCP support in agent-platform RFPs now that both sit under one neutral foundation.
