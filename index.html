⚡ Pattern of the Day
The constraints start generating their own market — power scarcity funds an off-grid build-out, multicloud gets a managed link, and the AI-Act Code gets a roster.

Yesterday the hardened constraints acquired hard dates. Today each one is spawning a productised response an architect can buy or decide on — the point at which a watch-list item becomes a procurement question. On power, the scarcity PJM and ERCOT ration is pulling capital off-grid: Enverus sees hyperscalers spending ~$5T through 2030 to build ~62 GW of behind-the-meter, gas-fired power, and EU projects (EdgeMode's €3B, 300 MW Spain campus) are now designed around on-site generation — even as PJM's comment window closes in ~11 days (3 September) ahead of the 12 October effective date.

On cloud portability, the 89%-multicloud reality gets a managed primitive: AWS and Google Cloud launched cross-cloud interconnect (GA) with an open interoperability specification and on-demand VPC links "in minutes," with Azure and OCI to follow. On agents, the identity plane that standardised yesterday gets its knowledge layer — Pinecone Nexus reached GA, exposing enterprise data as governed, agent-ready knowledge via one call, while Salesforce's Agentic Enterprise Index shows the fleet deepening (agents-per-org 5 → 13, ~7 in 10 service sessions autonomous). Regulation turned method into a roster: the Commission published a first list of 180+ signatories to the Transparency Code.

For Group Three moves: (1) with PJM inside two weeks, decide and insert self-supply/curtailment clauses in any PJM-footprint commitment now, and add a behind-the-meter-capability test to capacity diligence (weighed against AG's carbon commitments); (2) treat AWS–Google cross-cloud interconnect as a portability lever — validate latency, egress pricing and residency before routing anything sovereign across it; and (3) cross-reference AG's GPAI and generative-AI suppliers against the published 180+ signatory list, flag non-signatories for heightened diligence, sign the Code for AG's own in-scope systems, and pair "governed knowledge access + IdP-brokered MCP auth" as one agent-procurement requirement.
