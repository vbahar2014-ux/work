⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday the law went live and the standards bodies moved in. Today the capital that funds the whole cycle blinks — and agent governance ships as product.

The build-out shows its first financial crack: Blackstone unexpectedly walks away from what would be the world's largest data-centre campus, read by some as a sign the boom may be peaking — landing days after New York froze ~9.3 GW of pending capacity in the first statewide moratorium. Capital discipline now joins politics and grid as a limit on "infinite" compute, even as US YTD DC spend still runs ~4× last year and the Gulf scales (Damac Digital at 6,000 MW planned).

Meanwhile agent governance stops being a standards debate and becomes a shipping product: Google's expanded Gemini Enterprise sells the tooling to build, orchestrate and govern fleets of agents under IT control — the same identity/audit primitives the ITU is standardising and the EU is about to enforce. Anthropic, now the reported revenue leader (~$47B, profitable) hiring Karpathy and Blomfield, shows the supplier side consolidating. The EU clock ticks to ~6 / ~17 days.

For Group One item crosses into decision this cycle: lock the EU content-transparency Code sign / don't-sign decision by 22 July (~6 days), confirming whether Group's foundation-model providers sit on the ~24-strong GPAI Code signatory list (Meta absent, xAI partial). The Blackstone exit plus the NY freeze sharpen the case for European/Gulf-primary siting; the Gemini Enterprise launch makes the agent control-plane choice more urgent — align it to the emerging ITU FG-TIDA identity direction rather than a vendor default.
