⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday the theses were financed. Today they ship, run hot, and get gated.

Europe's sovereign-cloud thesis stopped being a spending line and became a bookable product: Evroc went live (3 Jul) with an NVIDIA Blackwell cluster (B200/L40S) and roc, a sovereign enterprise AI agent — sovereignty off the shelf, not just on a balance sheet. Meanwhile compute is consumed as fast as it is financed: US data-centre construction is running >4x last year's record ($58.1B YTD) and GB300 capacity is leased by the month (~$150M, up to $6.3B).

The frontier itself is now rationed: GPT-5.6 (Sol/Terra/Luna) was shared with the US government before release, capped at ~20 orgs, and Sol runs on Cerebras at ~750 tok/s. And the agent bottleneck moved from "which model" to governing thousands of them, with MCP consolidating as the interop standard — while the EU AI Act clock ticks: GPAI enforcement 2 Aug (~31 days), signatory window 22 Jul (~20 days).

For AG Sovereignty is now buy-vs-build, not just finance (Evroc) — sharpen the EU landing-zone call. Keep long-horizon capacity priced as scarce (18–24 mo out) and the agent fabric model-agnostic with routing, since frontier access can no longer be assumed. One call lands this cycle: the GPAI-Code sign / don't-sign decision by 22 Jul; carryover: re-validate M365/Copilot exposure now the new pricing is in effect.
