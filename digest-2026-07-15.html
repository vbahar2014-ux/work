⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday the governance of agents moved into product and into law. Today the law goes live, the standards bodies move in behind it — and the build-out hits its hardest political wall yet.

The conduct layer is real: China's Interim Measures for Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services take effect today, 15 Jul — the first major jurisdiction to regulate agents as a distinct class (scope narrowed to emotional-companion services; enterprise work assistants, customer service and knowledge Q&A are excluded). Behind it, the identity layer: the ITU stands up FG-TIDA to write reference architectures for agent identity, discovery, interoperability and lifecycle trust.

On the ground, New York becomes the first US state to pause new hyperscale data centres (one-year moratorium, ≥50 MW, 39 pending) — the siting backlash escalates from municipal to statewide even as US YTD DC spend hits $58.1B (~4× 2025) and Meta turns "Meta Compute" into a commercial cloud. The EU clock ticks to ~7 / ~18 days.

For Group The conduct layer is now law (though China's reach today is emotional-companion services, not Group's enterprise agents) and the identity layer is being standardised. Two items land this cycle: lock the EU content-transparency Code decision by 22 July (~7 days); and classify Group's agent estate against agent-specific rules — identity, human oversight, agent-disclosure — now emerging across the EU, India and China, aligning Group's agent-identity baseline to the ITU's direction.
