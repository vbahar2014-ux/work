⚡ Pattern of the Day
The agent wave got a price tag and a personality — on the same day the physical floor split by jurisdiction.

Yesterday the contest was which agent control plane. Today the wave is quantified — ~$206.5B of agent software in 2026, up ~139% YoY, the fastest-growing line in enterprise software — and the agents stop being stateless copilots: NVIDIA and ServiceNow ship "Project Arc," a long-running, self-evolving desktop agent. A standing agent with persistent permissions is a far larger identity and audit surface, and the industry's own answer is the AI-BOM — inventory every model, agent, dataset and prompt.

On the physical floor the story diverges by geography: where the US opened a 90-day grid fast-lane yesterday, Denmark's Energinet holds its moratorium on new large-load connections (60 GW queue vs ~7 GW peak demand), even as Microsoft lands a concrete 2 GW Texas campus. And the EU AI Act clock turned from a window into a date — GPAI + Art. 50 marking stay locked to 2 Aug (~35 days), with the Transparency Code's signatory list closing 22 July (~24 days).

For Group Pick the agent control-plane + identity/inventory standard before the next production rollout — now that agents self-evolve, governance is far harder to retrofit. And make the 22 July transparency-Code signing call deliberately, not by default.
