⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday the theses shipped and got gated. Today the gate lifts on the model tier — while it comes down on the physical one.

The efficient agentic tier just broadened: Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5 (1 Jul) — its most agentic model yet, near-frontier quality at ~$2/$10 per Mtok — and restored its top Fable/Mythos models after an 18-day US export-control pause. A direct sequel to yesterday's "frontier access is government-gated" thread, and a double argument for keeping AG's agent fabric model-agnostic with routing.

But the physical build-out now hits a wall that isn't supply: Virginia's first-in-US data-centre power tax ($0.011/kWh) goes live 1 Jul, and Monterey Park voters permanently ban data centres (Measure NDC, 88%). Meanwhile Gartner formalises the direction — 2026 supply-chain trends name agentic AI, physical AI, decision governance and product provenance as top — and the EU AI Act clock ticks: GPAI enforcement 2 Aug (~30 days), content-transparency Code signatory window 22 Jul (~19 days).

For AG Keep the agent fabric model-agnostic with routing — Claude Sonnet 5 shows a cheaper tier lands and gating is reversible. Price siting for fiscal + community risk, not only MW and $/kWh, now that power is taxed and zoned. One call lands this cycle: the sign / don't-sign decision against the content-transparency Code by 22 Jul; carryover: re-validate M365/Copilot exposure under the new pricing.
