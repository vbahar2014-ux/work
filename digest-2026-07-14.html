⚡ Pattern of the Day
Yesterday concentration became supervised and the evidence base cracked. Today the governance of agents moves off the whiteboard — into product and into law.

On product, the governance plane now ships with the agent: NVIDIA + ServiceNow's "Project Arc" (a governed long-running desktop agent), NVIDIA + LangChain's open Deep Agents blueprint for on-prem governed agents, and Quiq's "Verified Intelligence" supervisory control layer. On law, China becomes the first major jurisdiction to regulate AI agents as a distinct category — the Interim Measures for Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services take effect 15 Jul (tomorrow) — and the UN convenes its Global Dialogue on AI Governance in Geneva.

The EU clock ticks to ~8 / ~19 days. And the compute build-out that funds the whole cycle accelerates through the US siting backlash: Meta lifts its Louisiana "Hyperion" campus to 5 GW / $50B+ and stands up a "Meta Compute" unit — a reminder that power, not silicon, is the binding constraint.

For Group The regulated object is widening from AI-generated content to agent conduct. Two items land this cycle: lock the EU content-transparency Code decision by 22 July (~8 days); and confirm Group's agent inventory and classification anticipate agent-specific rules — identity, human oversight, agent-disclosure — now emerging across the EU, India and China, not just model-level GPAI.
