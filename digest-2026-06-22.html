⚡ Pattern of the Day
The week's story is codification. Two threads we tracked as sentiment hardened into structure — sovereignty became draft law, agent governance became a shipping product.

Sovereignty stopped being a survey statistic. The EU's Technological Sovereignty Package drafted the Cloud and AI Development Act — four named sovereignty assurance levels (L2 independence + supply-chain transparency, L3 EU-owned/controlled) and a plan to triple EU data-centre capacity.
