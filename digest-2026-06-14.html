⚡ Pattern of the Day
On a quiet Sunday the story is the agent stack's commercial and control terms — context becomes a metered utility, the verified-agent thesis gets a number, and governance becomes the named #1 barrier.

Microsoft's Work IQ context plane goes GA in two days, now shipping with Copilot-Credit spending limits and a cost dashboard — turning the substrate agents read identity, memory and context from into a billable, capped utility, so the plane Group standardises on sets both its audit model and its cost curve. Cloudflare puts a number under the same shift: a majority (51.7%) of AI-crawler traffic is now model-training — exactly the traffic its Web Bot Auth verified-agent gating exists to identify and price at the edge.

And governance has moved from "emerging vendor layer" to the primary barrier to scaling agents (Stanford 62%) with a named executive owner at most large enterprises (Forrester: 60% of Fortune 100). Around that core the build-out kept globalising — sovereign, AI-capable capacity reached West Africa (Kasi, Lagos) — while Gartner's 2026 set reads as one coherent agenda and the EU AI Act clock ticked to ~49 days with the Annex III deferral closer to law but still unbanked.

For Group The near-term call is no longer just which agent control plane — it is also how Group meters and caps agent context consumption (Work IQ's Copilot Credits make cost a live FinOps variable) and which standard governs verified-agent traffic at the edge. Decide the context-plane + credit-governance + identity-standard triad as one coupled choice before GA compounds usage.
