⚡ Pattern of the Day
The agent surface and its constraints both go horizontal — onto the phone, across the clouds, into the statehouse.

Last cycle agent governance got absorbed into the platform (Google's GA Agent Identity). This cycle the same forces spread sideways. The interaction surface widened again: after driving the desktop browser, Google's consumer agents now place phone calls to stores to check stock and complete purchases — a second credential-bearing channel to govern. The control-plane buy option answered the platform default: Cequence's AI Gateway added AI Discovery and API/LLM/Skill registries that surface every agent and MCP server already running from existing security logs — turning the "agent inventory" AG flagged as a design gate into a shippable product. The interop layer hardened too, with MCP shipping a major release candidate as enterprises use it to broker agent access to production systems.

Around the agent surface, its two constraints also went horizontal. Connectivity: Cloudflare reports machines now generate more than half of all traffic on its network — the "Agentic Internet" — with agentic AI traffic up ~1,700% YoY. Portability: AWS and Google shipped a vendor-neutral multicloud Interconnect (Apache-2.0 spec on GitHub, Azure joining) — a rare win against lock-in. Power: the binding constraint turned political — Texas paused all ERCOT data-center interconnections pending a state audit (BNEF: ~49.8 GW / ~20% of the US pipeline at risk), New York's EO 62 froze ≥50 MW hyperscale permits, and the PUCT votes on a good-cause exception on 20 Aug. Regulation stayed live: the EU AI Act is ~17 days into enforcement, and the Digital Omnibus (in force 27 Jul) is confirmed to leave the 2 Aug GPAI date standing while banking the Annex III (2 Dec 2027) and Annex I (2 Aug 2028) deferrals.

For Group Two moves this cycle: (1) pilot Cequence AI Discovery to stand up AG's agent + MCP inventory as a design-review gate, reconciled against AG's portable identity standard; and (2) keep the EU AI Act operating posture running (confirm which GPAI suppliers are Code-of-Practice signatories) and add interconnection-pause risk to any US capacity diligence ahead of the 20 Aug PUCT vote.
