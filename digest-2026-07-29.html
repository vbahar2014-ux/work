⚡ Pattern of the Day
The countdown narrows to four days — Meta joins the list and the build-out faces its verdict.

Meta joins Google on the signatory list as the EU clock enters its final days: Meta confirmed on 28 Jul it is signing the content-transparency Code, becoming the second named flagship on the published initial-signatory list, while GPAI enforcement (fines up to €15M / 3% turnover), Article 50 marking and Member-State AI sandboxes all activate 2 August — now ~4 days out. That is the single item that crosses from awareness into decision this cycle.

Tomorrow the money behind the build-out faces its test: AWS reports Q2 after the close (30 Jul) with 2026 capex guided to ~$200B (AWS alone ~$165B, +70%) and a guide-raise expected, while Google Cloud's backlog has nearly doubled to ~$460B — the read on whether a trillion-dollar spend is pre-sold. And the physical limit answers back: new data warns 30–50% of 2026 data-centre capacity could slip on power and construction bottlenecks, with DC electricity on course to quadruple by 2035.

For Group One action this cycle: confirm which of Group's foundation-model providers appear on the published GPAI / Code signatory list (a proxy for Article 50 presumption-of-conformity), and finish the AI-generated-content marking inventory to a multi-jurisdiction, agent-aware standard before 2 August — staffed to deliver, not merely funded, on four days' notice. The agent-native-cloud pattern now reaches AMD silicon (TokenVisor Spaces) — a hardware-diverse second source worth tracking as the reference, even where the provider is not.
