⚡ Pattern of the Day
The perimeter that authenticates and inspects is itself the way in.

Issue #71, covering the ~48 hours since Issue #70. In seven days AG's suppliers produced two maximum-severity, unauthenticated-root zero-days in exactly the appliances that stand at the edge and decide who is trusted: Cisco Secure Email Gateway (CVE-2026-76461, deadline today) and now Identity Services Engine (CVE-2026-76460, CVSS 10.0, deadline Friday). Neither was found by proactive research — SEG was already being exploited on disclosure, and ISE surfaced only from a customer support ticket, meaning at least one enterprise was compromised before the advisory existed.

Disclosure-to-KEV compression is no longer the story; pre-disclosure compromise of identity and mail infrastructure is. On the strategic side, Dreamforce closed (15–17 Sept) with the agent-control plane fully productized — AIforce, the Nvidia-built Koa CRM reasoning model, Claudeforce in public beta, and every frontier model on Agentforce via Amazon Bedrock — while the HBM memory floor under any on-prem AI build stays elevated (Micron shipping 48GB 16-high samples, doubling capacity).

For Group Two clocks, two owners. On the defensive clock, set by attackers: patch Identity Services Engine to a fixed release before 19 Sept (no workaround), confirm Secure Email Gateway on AsyncOS 16.5.0-780 today, finish the Chrome fleet (18 Sept) and the September Windows cumulative incl. RDS (22 Sept), and — because both Cisco flaws were found only when a victim surfaced — hunt the identity and mail tiers rather than trusting version strings. On the strategic clock, set by vendors' calendars: read the AIforce / agent-control plane against AG's own standard before committing, treat Claudeforce's public beta as an evaluation not a default, and hold the elevated-HBM assumption in on-prem AI TCO. Standing watch: EU AI Act 2 Dec marking (~76 days); CRA reporting in force.

Patch Cisco Identity Services Engine to a fixed release (CVE-2026-76460, CVSS 10.0) before 19 Sept — an unauthenticated API auth-bypass giving remote attackers root, confirmed exploited, found in a customer support case, with no workaround

Suggested action · Upgrade ISE / ISE-PIC to a fixed patch level (3.5 P4, 3.4 P7, 3.3 P12, 3.2 P11, 3.1 P12) before 19 Sept. Consequence of inaction: ISE is the network-access-control and identity-policy plane — root on it lets an attacker mint access, disable enforcement and erase their tracks across the estate, a compromise patching alone will not remediate once exposure predates the fix.

📡 Radar — early & longer-horizon signals
Pre-disclosure compromise of identity/mail infrastructure
Cisco's ISE 10.0 was found only in a customer support ticket, and the SEG 9.8 was already exploited on disclosure — both edge appliances were breached before defenders knew. The story is no longer disclosure-to-KEV speed but victim-first discovery. Raises the cost of every open identity, mail and management exposure. Next trigger: first in-the-wild use of an autonomously-discovered zero-day, or a named AG-sector victim.
Türkiye sovereign cloud — Türksat Gölbaşı + Turkcell/Google $3B region
The Gölbaşı national DC (35,300 m², 8×+ capacity, domestic software for e-Devlet, service 2027) and the Turkcell–Google Cloud $3B hyperscale region in Ankara (build from Q1 2026, full capacity 2028) frame Türkiye's data-residency trajectory. Watched for AG's Türkiye data-residency posture. Next trigger: a firm commissioning date, KVKK localization decision, or public-sector cloud procurement rule.

Feeds the carryover sovereignty decision matrix. Next trigger: a CADA implementing step, or an EU-boundary AI-processing assurance AG can test.
Data-center liquid-cooling supply consolidates

Watched for AG capacity-build supply exposure. Next trigger: a lead-time or pricing signal on liquid-cooling components.
