⚡ Pattern of the Day
The hardened constraints acquire hard dates — Texas puts 10 December on the audit, PJM's window is inside two weeks, and the AI-Act marking route locks.

Yesterday's cycle was about the machinery starting: FERC docketing PJM's plans, ERCOT saying it would audit "shortly after" the 20 Aug meeting, Microsoft shipping agent governance as product. Today each thread gets a calendar. On power, ERCOT now plans to complete Governor Abbott's data-center audit by 10 December 2026, verifying ~250–300 Batch Zero large loads, with the first RFIs going out as early as end-August and rounds in Oct–Nov before a report to the PUCT a week before its December meeting. PJM's parallel clock is now inside two weeks — the 3 September comment window closes ahead of the requested 12 October effective date — and the backlash widened to New York (≥50 MW permit pause) and North Carolina (electricity tax exemption repealed).

On agents, the identity-plane decision went standards-level and multi-vendor: the new MCP 2026-07-28 specification makes the protocol stateless and moves authorization into the enterprise identity provider, Cequence shipped AI Discovery that inventories every agent/LLM/MCP server from existing logs, and Cloudflare named Mesh, a private-networking fabric for agents. Regulation hardened from a deadline into a settled method — the Commission's adequacy decision on the final Transparency Code of Practice plus the final Article 50 guidelines make signing the Code the recognised route to the 2 December 2026 marking deadline for pre-existing generative AI (~102 days), with GPAI enforcement ~20 days in.

For Group Three moves: (1) treat the ERCOT 10 Dec audit as a schedulable dependency — prepare Batch Zero RFI responses now, price the Sept–Dec slip into Texas commitments, and require self-supply/curtailment clauses in the PJM footprint before 12 Oct; (2) specify the agent-identity baseline as procurement, not build — require IdP-brokered MCP auth + a discovery sweep of existing logs (Microsoft/Cloudflare/Cequence interchangeable) and keep the agent + MCP inventory board-reportable; and (3) adopt the Transparency Code of Practice as AG's Article 50 conformity path now — mark new agent output immediately, schedule the pre-existing generative estate against 2 Dec 2026.
